Buys a coin trading at least 1.0 standard deviations below its recent average, provided the bounce on offer at least covers the round trip in fees and slippage (about 1.65%). Tuned for the maximum trade frequency this strategy can produce - roughly 79% of days get a trade, longest gap 5 days - rather than for returns: a full parameter sweep found no profitable setting of this strategy at Kraken's 0.80%-per-side taker fee, so these settings are expected to lose roughly 20% a year. Position size is held to 2% of equity so the ledger survives that. The momentum strategy in this repo measures far better but trades only about 7-30 times a year - see docs/momentum_research_2026-09-19.md.
